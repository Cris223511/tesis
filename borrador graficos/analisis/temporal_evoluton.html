Figure 9. Temporal Evolution of ML Model Family Implementations (2020-2025)

Note: The chart displays the annual distribution of published studies across six ML model families over the 2020-2025 period (n = 42 total studies). A marked concentration emerges in 2022-2024 (76.2% of all publications), reflecting rapid maturation of mobile ML frameworks and increased research focus on accessible ASD interventions. Wearable/Mobile and Traditional ML families maintain consistent presence across years, while CNN/Deep Learning implementations accelerate notably from 2023 onward, suggesting growing adoption of deep learning approaches in clinical ASD contexts.
