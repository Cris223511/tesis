Mean emotion recognition accuracy across different ASD severity levels in pediatric participants. ASD Level 1 shows highest mean accuracy (80.2%), followed by Asperger Syndrome (71.7%), while Level 3 shows lowest (42.8%). Data shows inverse correlation between ASD severity and recognition capacity.

Frequency distribution of predominant emotions detected during field validation with 49 pediatric participants with ASD across 79 therapeutic sessions. Neutral is most frequent (24.05%), followed by Angry (21.52%) and Disgusted (18.99%). Happy and Sad both represent 17.72%.

Mean predominance percentage for each emotion across sessions where that emotion was identified as predominant in pediatric participants with ASD.
